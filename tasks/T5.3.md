## Goal
Implement end-to-end differential verification proving that for identical input vectors and weights:
$$Y_F == Y_P == Y_{\text{ref\_int64}}$$
across at least 5 distinct matrix and tile shapes:
1. Toy shape ($1 \times 3$, tile $1 \times 3$)
2. Symmetric square multiple shape ($8 \times 8$, tile $4 \times 4$)
3. Rectangular multiple shape ($16 \times 32$, tile $8 \times 16$)
4. Non-multiple shape ($5 \times 11$, tile $4 \times 4$ with zero padding)
5. Real SmolLM2 slice shape (e.g. $8 \times 16$ tile from Layer 0 `q_proj`)
