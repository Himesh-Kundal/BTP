## Goal
Implement testbench generation and vector dumping for Design P:
1. `write_weight_hex_vectors`: dumps $N' \times K'$ weights into `w.hex` in row-major order (2 hex characters per INT8 signed byte).
2. `generate_wrapper_testbench_p`: emits self-checking testbench that:
   - Resets the DUT with `rst_n`.
   - Executes the weight-load phase: drives `w_we = 1`, streams `w_addr` from $0$ to $N' \cdot K' - 1$ with `w_data = w_mem[addr]`, then sets `w_we = 0`.
   - Streams input activations `x_flat` with `valid_in`, checks `valid_out` after latency $L=1$, and compares `y_flat` with `y_exp.hex`.
   - Emits standardized summary line: `TB_RESULT CHECKED=<n> MISMATCH=<m> TIMING_ERR=<t>`.
3. Verify simulation of toy core and real SmolLM2 tile in Icarus Verilog (`iverilog`/`vvp`).
