## Goal
Demonstrate compiler versatility and scalability across $\ge 3$ distinct $(N, K)$ matrix shapes, including a full-layer RTL generation:
1. Shape 1: Full-layer projection ($576 \times 576$, e.g. SmolLM2 layer 0 `q_proj`).
   - 2,592 tiles of $8 \times 16$.
   - Generates complete full-layer RTL within the frozen time (<1s) and size (<10MB) budgets.
   - Audits weight constants against original $576 \times 576$ matrix.
2. Shape 2: Non-multiple arbitrary rectangular shape (e.g. $19 \times 37$ with synthetic weights and bias).
   - Verifies zero-padding logic: $19 \to 24$ (3 tiles of 8), $37 \to 48$ (3 tiles of 16).
   - Validates bit-exact simulation match on unpadded outputs against independent reference oracle.
3. Shape 3: Asymmetric wide projection (e.g. $32 \times 128$ slice or $192 \times 576$ `k_proj`).
   - Multiple column reduction tree.
   - Differential simulation match `Y_F == Y_ref`.
4. Output structured evidence to `results/demo.json`.
