## Goal

1. Synthesize an identical $(N_t, K_t)$ tile across 6 weight distribution classes:

   - **Magnitude-matched random** (same histogram of $|w|$ as real weights, but randomized signs and locations).
   - **Shuffled real weights** (identical weight values with randomly permuted $(n, k)$ positions).
   - **All-zero weights** (extreme sparsity baseline).
   - **One-hot weights** (single nonzero coefficient per row).
2. Measure cell counts, gate equivalents, and LUT4 counts for each class.
3. Save structured data to `results/E2.csv`.
