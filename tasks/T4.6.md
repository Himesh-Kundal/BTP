## Goal
Implement interface conformance tests and the weight port audit in `verification/weight_audit.py` to statically verify generated RTL:
1. Asserts wrapper ports strictly match `(clk, rst_n, valid_in, x_flat, valid_out, y_flat)`.
2. Proves zero weight ports, zero `w_*` signals, and zero writable storage exist in Design F.
3. Negative control: verifies that injecting a fake weight port or `w_*` signal causes the audit to immediately FAIL.

## Acceptance (executable)
- [ ] `pytest tests/test_interface_conformance.py` passes
- [ ] Conformance test checks signal names, directions, and bit widths against manifest.json
- [ ] Negative control: injecting a synthetic port `input [7:0] w_data` or `reg [7:0] w_reg` triggers audit failure
- [ ] Audit passes on all generated Design F tiles and top-level modules
